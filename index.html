Privacy Policy

Poker Lineup · Last updated: 17 October 2026

Poker Lineup is a note-taking app for live poker players. It lets you record what you observe about opponents and find those notes again later. This page explains what data the app handles, why, and what your rights are. We kept it short and plain on purpose.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Poker Lineup (publisher details to be completed before release: legal name and address).

Contact for any privacy request: contact@poker-lineup.com

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: your email address and a password. The password is stored only in hashed form, so nobody, including us, can read it.To create your account and sign you in.
Your notes about opponents: a first name or nickname, optional physical traits (gender, hair, build, distinguishing mark, accessories), an optional nationality, free-text details, general tendencies, and dated preflop and postflop reads.This is the purpose of the app: to capture and find your notes.
Your tables: which seats are taken, when, and the stake you selected.To place your notes on the table you are playing.
Technical data: IP address and timestamps recorded by our hosting provider when you sign in or use the service.Security and keeping the service working.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking code.

Notes about other people

Your opponent notes describe other people. They are your own personal notes, and you decide what to write. Please do not record sensitive information about anyone (for example health, ethnic origin, religion or political opinions). We never use these notes for anything other than showing them back to you, and we do not combine them into statistics, rankings or profiles.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication), in a data centre in the European Union (Paris). Supabase acts on our behalf as our processor. Your data is never sold and never shared for advertising.

The app also keeps a copy of your notes on your own device, so it keeps working without a connection. That copy and your sign-in session use your device's local storage. No advertising or analytics cookies are used, so there is no cookie banner.

Legal basis

We process your account and notes because it is needed to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract). We process technical data for security, which is our legitimate interest.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all your notes are erased immediately. Copies in the hosting provider's backups, if any, are overwritten on a rolling basis.

To delete your account: open the app, tap the power icon on the table screen, then Account and Delete account, and type DELETE to confirm. This cannot be undone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a common format, restrict or object to its processing. You can edit and delete your notes in the app at any time. For anything else, including a copy of your data, email us at contact@poker-lineup.com. We will answer within one month.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to your data protection authority. In France, that is the CNIL.

Age

Poker Lineup is meant for adults. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its date. We will tell you in the app before any change that affects you significantly.
